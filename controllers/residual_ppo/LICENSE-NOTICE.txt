Smart Buildings Residual RL - licence notice

Everything in this dataset is released under Apache-2.0.

It builds on:
- zt_controller.py: the per-zone PI controller "zt" by malyshevdanil, Apache-2.0
  (https://www.kaggle.com/code/malyshevdanil/smart-buildings-probe-zt-best). Changed: an off-hours rule and research knobs added.
- base.py: wraps the organisers' PIDWaterResetPolicy from the NeurIPS 2026 Smart Buildings starter kit, Apache-2.0.
The weights in skynet.pt were trained by us on the competition simulator.
